### 1. A single blood protein predicts dementia, depression, Parkinson's and stroke — but probably doesn't cause them

**PMID:** 42571855 | **DOI:** 10.1016/j.pnpbp.2026.111886

**Media check:** Not widely covered ✓ (SERPAPI verified)

**The study:** Using UK Biobank data from a large prospective cohort followed for a median of 14 years, Chinese researchers examined whether baseline plasma levels of GDF15 — a stress-responsive protein — were associated with developing eight brain disorders. ### 2. Cutting and repairing a hand nerve reshuffles the brain's finger map — but surprisingly doesn't explain why patients struggle

**Media check:** Not widely covered ✓ (SERPAPI verified)

**The study:** Using functional MRI, researchers mapped how the primary somatosensory cortex (S1) represents individual fingers in 21 patients who had undergone surgical hand nerve repair and 30 healthy controls. Crucially, the researchers found no credible links between the degree of map disruption and patients' actual functional impairments, suggesting the cortical circuitry itself may remain largely intact despite the disordered inputs.
