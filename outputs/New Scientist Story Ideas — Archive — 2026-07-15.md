**Media check:** Not widely covered ✓ (SERPAPI verified)

**The study:** A large multinational genomic study applied structural equation modelling to genome-wide association data from up to 933,970 individuals across five psychiatric disorders and three insulin-resistance conditions. The finding identifies 128 previously unknown genes and points to prenatal brain development as a key window, with potential implications for identifying new drug targets. **Media check:** Not widely covered ✓ (SERPAPI verified)

**The study:** In a controlled laboratory study at the University of Missouri, 78 young adult moderate drinkers (55% female, mean age 23.9) were administered enough alcohol to reach a breath alcohol concentration of 0.10% — above the US legal driving limit — and then completed measures of alcohol-impaired driving (AID) risk perception. Researchers tested how five facets of trait impulsivity (the UPPS-P model) related to risk perceptions across the intoxication curve. This isn't just impulsivity amplifying risk-taking; it's impulsivity warping reality. The finding could reshape how we design interventions for repeat drink-drivers, who may not be ignoring danger so much as failing to see it.
