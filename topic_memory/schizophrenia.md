# Topic Memory: schizophrenia
_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

## Established findings
- Severe mental illness carries a large excess-mortality burden that public health tools have not closed; England-wide records show a ~56% higher post-covid-infection death risk and a gap that widened during and after vaccine roll-out (PMID 42586081, Sep 2026).
- Antipsychotic-induced metabolic harm is a central driver of the life expectancy gap; semaglutide currently ranks first among 39 candidate drugs for weight reduction in schizophrenia spectrum disorders (PMID 42418179, Sep 2026).
- The strict dopamine "final common pathway" model is being formally bounded by senior figures in the field, citing non-dopaminergic treatment response (xanomeline-trospium) and dopamine-normal treatment resistance (PMID 42418174, Sep 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Measurement mismatch as a recurring result: momentary/experience-sampling data failing to reproduce a positive trial's interview-based benefit (PMID 42658758); clinician suicide risk ratings highly specific but low-sensitivity across 249,011 records (PMID 42044698); empathy scales with poor internal consistency (PMID 42664611); no replicated predictors of who benefits from CBT for psychosis in 23 studies (PMID 42679421). Strong candidate for a future feature.
- Biomarker direction reversals and trajectory-over-snapshot logic: 40 Hz auditory steady-state response *larger*, not smaller, in non-remitting clinical high-risk youth (PMID 42274463); unvalidated striatal connectivity prediction of 12-week antipsychotic response (PMID 42574776).
- Avatar therapy maturing: multi-site extension outside the originating lab with uneven response and low dropout (PMID 42475895), plus an fMRI dissociation suggesting it engages amygdala/dmPFC social-threat circuitry rather than the hallucination-generating language regions (PMID 42334305).
- Aging with psychosis as a services problem: pooled 24.3% dementia prevalence in older adults with psychotic disorders, very high heterogeneity (PMID 42615098).
- Motor signs returning as a clinical marker: hyperkinetic severity at two months predicting verbal memory and functioning at two years in first-episode psychosis (PMID 42640953).
- Etiological negatives worth watching: temperament and adverse childhood experiences predicted alcohol use, bipolar and OCD but not schizophrenia (AUC 0.526) in Indian multiplex families (PMID 42680113).
- Stress-axis epigenetics: FKBP5 enhancer hypomethylation converging with age and rs1360780 in postmortem frontal cortex (PMID 42642806).
- Evidence blind spots in reproductive and sexual health: first cariprazine breast-milk measurements (N=5, PMID 42085651); prolactin rise tracking sexual function within individuals but not between drugs (PMID 42439529).

## Feature ideas already pitched
