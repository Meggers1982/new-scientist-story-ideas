# Topic Memory: schizophrenia
_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

## Established findings
- Antipsychotic-induced cardiometabolic harm drives the life expectancy gap: semaglutide and metformin are the only agents with meaningful weight effects (PMID 42418179), metformin but not adjunctive aripiprazole flattens weight on clozapine (PMID 42742489), and Mendelian randomization now implicates clozapine exposure itself in type 2 diabetes and raised blood pressure (PMID 42472444, Oct 2026).
- Group-level biomarkers repeatedly fail at individual prediction: unvalidated striatal connectivity models (PMID 42574776); prospective negative for EEG slowing as a clozapine response predictor (PMID 41124326).
- The strict dopamine "final common pathway" model is being formally bounded by senior figures (PMID 42418174).
- Severe mental illness carries large excess mortality not closed by public health tools, including ~56% higher post-covid-infection death risk in England (PMID 42586081).

## Emerging threads
- Heterogeneity beats averaging: personal recovery has no single predictor structure across latent classes (PMID 42734987, Sep 2026); computational memory parameters are reliable above 0.80 (PMID 42765980) while interview, empathy and risk-rating measures are not (PMIDs 42658758, 42664611, 42044698).
- Sex- and state-dependent genetic effects on cognition: NSF rs9896243 female verbal memory advantage present in siblings and controls, absent in first-episode patients (PMID 42843653); 22q11.2 profile stable in shape but lower in older age groups, n=1328 (PMID 42765380).
- Phenomenology and first-person work expanding: self-disturbance mediating neglect to psychotic experiences (PMID 42740395); negotiated autonomy with voices (PMID 41354941); evanescence versus excess of reality in schizophrenia and BPD (PMID 42853770).
- Patient-defined outcomes largely unmeasured by services: intimacy and romance as the recovery yardstick in early psychosis programs (PMID 41891676).
- Deprescribing and timing: pooled IPD find no distinct withdrawal-driven rapid relapse, baseline severity the predictor (PMID 42636852); delay to clozapine predicts unrecovered function (PMID 42155318).
- Excitation/inhibition imbalance via new routes: 40 Hz ASSR in non-remitting high-risk youth (PMID 42274463); aperiodic MEG exponent tracking symptoms and autobiographical memory (PMID 42744260).
- Case finding and automated sensing: one-item perceptual-disturbance screen at military discharge, 10% positive (PMID 42723010); gaze aversion timed to partners' smiles explaining reduced mimicry (PMID 42702091); acoustic markers specific to schizophrenia (PMID 42685439).
- Schizophrenia as public fear rather than diagnosis: near-universal intrusive thoughts about going mad, driven by beliefs about losing control (PMID 42800306).
- Microbiome work still confounded by medication (PMIDs 42596905, 42758012).
