# Topic Memory: schizophrenia
_Last updated: 2026-09-23_

## Established findings
- Antipsychotic-induced metabolic harm is a central driver of the life expectancy gap; semaglutide and metformin are the only agents with clinically meaningful weight effects (PMID 42418179, Sep 2026), and metformin — not adjunctive aripiprazole — flattens weight trajectories on clozapine in real-world care (PMID 42742489, Sep 2026).
- Severe mental illness carries a large excess-mortality burden that public health tools have not closed; England-wide records show ~56% higher post-covid-infection death risk, widening during and after vaccine roll-out (PMID 42586081, Sep 2026).
- The strict dopamine "final common pathway" model is being formally bounded by senior figures, citing non-dopaminergic treatment response and dopamine-normal treatment resistance (PMID 42418174, Sep 2026).
- Group-level biomarkers repeatedly fail at individual prediction: unvalidated striatal connectivity models (PMID 42574776), and a prospective negative for EEG slowing as a clozapine response predictor (PMID 41124326, Sep 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Measurement and reliability as the field's live problem — experience sampling not reproducing interview-based trial benefit (PMID 42658758), low-sensitivity clinician suicide risk ratings (PMID 42044698), poor empathy scales (PMID 42664611), no replicated predictors of CBT response (PMID 42679421) — now with a rare positive: computational memory parameters reliable above 0.80 (PMID 42765980, Sep 2026).
- Excitation/inhibition imbalance measured by new routes: enlarged 40 Hz ASSR in non-remitting high-risk youth (PMID 42274463); aperiodic MEG exponent tracking symptoms and autobiographical memory (PMID 42744260).
- Deprescribing and timing of antipsychotics: pooled IPD from five trials find no distinct withdrawal-driven rapid relapse, with baseline severity the predictor (PMID 42636852); delay to clozapine predicts unrecovered function (PMID 42155318).
- First-person and phenomenological work gaining traction: need for a believable, bearable explanation of hallucinations (PMID 42627099); autonomy as continuously negotiated with voices (PMID 41354941); self-disturbance as a mediator from neglect to psychotic experiences (PMID 42740395).
- Contested role of childhood adversity: null for schizophrenia in multiplex families (PMID 42680113) versus full mediation of abuse via negative self-beliefs in the GROUP cohort (PMID 42740395).
- Automated behavioral sensing: acoustic markers specific to schizophrenia but not schizotypal PD (PMID 42685439); gaze aversion timed to partners' smiles explaining reduced mimicry (PMID 42702091).
- Aging and development at high genetic risk: 24.3% pooled dementia prevalence in older adults with psychosis (PMID 42615098); 22q11.2 cognitive profile stable in shape but lower in older age groups, n=1328 (PMID 42765380).
- Case finding and services: one-item perceptual-disturbance screen at military discharge flags 10% positive, with undetected attenuated and threshold psychosis (PMID 42723010).
- Microbiome in psychiatry still confounded by medication: transient probiotic effect on olanzapine metabolic indices (PMID 42596905); reduced diversity in clozapine-treated treatment-resistant patients with no unmedicated comparator (PMID 42758012).
