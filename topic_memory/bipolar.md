# Topic Memory: bipolar
_Last updated: 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- Severe mental illness carries a large, early physical-health penalty: chronic disease accumulates across multiple organ systems from young adulthood, worst in women under 25 (PMID 42586082, Sep 2026).
- Crude inflammatory markers do not distinguish bipolar disorder from schizophrenia at first episode (PMID 42112712, Sep 2026); diagnostic category maps poorly onto brain measures generally (PMIDs 42679550, 42779463).
- Bipolar disorder shares genetic architecture with substance *dependence*, not substance *use* as such (PMID 42636965, Aug 2026).
- Internalized stigma harms functioning in remitted bipolar disorder via perceived devaluation/discrimination, not coping, insight, self-esteem or support (PMID 42113129, Sep 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Lithium's special status is under pressure: no advantage over valproate for suicide in cohort plus network meta-analysis (PMID 42765379); meanwhile a peripheral transcriptomic signature tied to white matter is proposed as a lithium-response readout (PMID 42742966).
- Off-label antipsychotic prescribing in intellectual disability now has within-person outcome data showing harm without behavioral benefit (PMID 42711066) — watch for STOMP-related follow-up and for replication outside Hong Kong.
- Large territory-wide pharmacoepidemiology (Hong Kong Hospital Authority, Queensland registries) is becoming the default source for questions no trial will answer.
- "Biotype" reclassification of psychosis: B-SNIP-style perfusion work dissociates trait (low CBF) from state (hyperperfusion tracking symptoms) (PMID 42779463) — still nothing clinic-ready.
- Digital thread shifting from prediction models (PMID 42600789) to passive sensing of mood–behavior loops (PMID 42765378) and to co-designed AI companions for people with serious mental illness (PMID 42789145, author holds equity — flag).
- Endocannabinoid reset after mania is blocked in cannabis users (PMID 42735776) — a state-level candidate mechanism complementing the dependence-genetics account.
- Evidence-credibility auditing (umbrella reviews grading whole literatures) is gaining ground; eating-disorder comorbidity prevalence holds up, risk/outcome estimates do not (PMID 42777340).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-07: "The research double" — psychiatric evidence built on tidy samples fails on real, comorbid patients, across trial eligibility, biomarkers, psychotherapy and prediction algorithms (PMIDs 42575197, 42173043, 41084836, 41904070, 42600789).
- 2026-09-27: The physical-health debt of serious mental illness — early multi-organ multimorbidity, off-label injectable harms, the weakened case for lithium's monitoring burden, and movement as the one lever (PMIDs 42586082, 42711066, 42765379, 42765378).
