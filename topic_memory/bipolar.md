# Topic Memory: bipolar
_Last updated: 2026-09-07_

## Established findings
- Bipolar disorder shares genetic architecture with substance *dependence* rather than substance *use* per se; dopamine and interneuron pathways implicated (PMID 42636965, Aug 2026).
- Inflammatory markers in routine blood counts are raised in first-episode psychosis but do not distinguish bipolar disorder from schizophrenia (PMID 42112712, Sep 2026).
- Internalized stigma harms functioning in remitted bipolar disorder specifically via perceived devaluation/discrimination; coping, insight, self-esteem and social support do not mediate (PMID 42113129, Sep 2026).

## Emerging threads
- The "clean sample" problem: trial-eligibility gap in bipolar depression (PMID 42575197), comorbidity confounding a serotonin biomarker (PMID 42173043), comorbid PTSD not worsening DBT outcomes (PMID 41084836).
- "Treatment-resistant depression" may split into a complicated-but-responsive group (TRD1) and a genuinely blunted one (TRD2) — relevant to ketamine/ECT/neuromodulation eligibility (PMID 41904070).
- Clinical AI in psychiatry: dataset shift beats model sophistication; a parent-report screener (PGBI-10M) outperforms deep learning (PMID 42600789).
- Bipolar vs schizophrenia differentiation is failing at the crude-biomarker level but showing promise in hippocampal long-axis, task-based connectivity (PMID 42679550).
- Sex-stratified antipsychotic dosing is near-absent; two-injection LAI aripiprazole start in women (PMID 41924987) is unusual for being all-female — watch for replication.
- Vestibular stimulation altering self-face recognition (PMID 42276516) — a possible mechanism for depersonalization in chronic dizziness; watch for follow-up in patient groups.

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-07: "The research double" — psychiatric evidence built on tidy samples fails on real, comorbid patients, traced across trial eligibility, biomarkers, psychotherapy and prediction algorithms (PMIDs 42575197, 42173043, 41084836, 41904070, 42600789).
