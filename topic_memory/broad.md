# Topic Memory: broad mind and brain
_Last updated: 2026-08-28_

## Established findings
- Widely promoted psychological interventions repeatedly fail or backfire in controlled tests: relaxation/cognitive reframing components (42546730), psychological first aid (42561812), mindfulness as a buffer (42622844, 42612528), symptom reframing (42629541), intensive self-monitoring (42606857). Recurred across 2026-08-22 and 2026-08-28 runs.
- Real-world antipsychotic prescribing diverges from guidelines: rising polypharmacy and low clozapine use in Hong Kong (42551239), 18% off-label clozapine across 55 countries (42628494).
- Precision psychiatry tools consistently judged not clinic-ready: readiness audit (42657684) against connectome suicide "fingerprints" (42612815), OxSATS clinician concerns (42618280), pharmacogenetic genotype-phenotype gap (42612943).
- Small single-center EEG cohorts keep reporting counterintuitive, unvalidated prognostic signatures at AUC ~0.83 (post-stroke 42663449; early-onset Alzheimer's 42617666).

## Emerging threads
- Iatrogenic harm inside psychotherapy as a research topic in its own right — recovered memories still occurring at 8–12% in therapy started 2006–2024 (42663614).
- "Dismantling" / component-level evaluation of interventions, including mediation of parenting programs (42613957) and Bayesian component NMA (42546730).
- Grief science as distinct from mood: closure null across the MH17 trial (42635331); gendered grief symptom networks with identity disruption central for men (42635333).
- Hypoxia deflated as a lever on brain state — null for circadian shift (42630089) and for depressive symptoms in sleep apnea (42663413); insomnia carries the signal instead.
- Autism agenda shifting from prevalence counting (42613816) to environment and communication effort (42547992) — sensory design angle underexploited.
- Basic cognition strand worth keeping alive: featureless object tracking (42537447), reasoning over working memory in speech-in-noise (42659749), chunk-boundary representations (42552609).
