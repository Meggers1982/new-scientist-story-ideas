# Topic Memory: memory
_Last updated: 2026-10-09_

## Established findings
- Enactment/physical rehearsal beats mental simulation for intentions and instructions (42627881, 42622513; Aug 2026), but enactment is also the failure point in a second language (42717310; Sep 2026).
- Offloading is double-edged: expected external access suppresses encoding (42622798), yet shunting items into a motor routine expands effective span (42754727; Sep 2026).
- Dual-task/eye-movement recall reduces vividness and unpleasantness of aversive memories, emotion-generally (42622123, 42531952; Aug 2026).
- Motivational state is a reliable encoding lever with physiological corroboration: volition over instructed control (42753975; Sep 2026) and monetary incentive, undiminished by age and mediated by anticipatory arousal (42849577; Oct 2026).
- Measurement validity keeps limiting translation: group-level EEG biomarkers unusable per child (42721928), response shift in long-term patient-reported outcomes (42727324), adult questionnaires on child executive function tracking task performance only for more educated parents (42166276).

## Emerging threads
- Core clinical trauma-memory theory under sustained attack: intrusions are not cleanly cue-specific (42759257); first human evidence that a threat-memory circuit (PVT) discriminates only at 7-10 days, not during learning (42843708) — the "24-hour problem" in extinction research.
- Public memory misinformation as a research object: professionals on TikTok endorse repression/recovery more than laypeople (42847121; Oct 2026). New in Oct 2026, watch for follow-ups on viewer belief.
- Preregistered nulls keep retiring popular claims: no cultural offset in earliest memory (42757868), intact attentional boost in depression (42706587), no cost of post-study mind wandering at 15 min (42771053), no causal link from induced loneliness to hostile attribution (42831817).
- Offline consolidation window contested: protective framing in epilepsy/insomnia (42748517, 42537590) versus a flat null over short waking delays (42771053). Timescale is the unresolved variable.
- Voluntary forgetting is content-dependent: survival-relevant food words resist forget instructions, death words show a recognition advantage instead (42844925).
- State-versus-trait in social cognition: positive-memory dampening shifts belief rigidity (42727265) but induced loneliness does not shift hostile attribution (42831817).
- Exposure attribution in brain-injury cohorts: football years act directly, military service only via PTSD (42753419).
