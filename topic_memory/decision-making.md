## Emerging threads
- Signal-detection/computational decomposition repeatedly finds intact perception or valuation in autism and autistic traits, with differences confined to decision criterion or choice rule: PMIDs 42721846, 42721950, 42722131 (Sep 2026). Watch for replication in diagnosed rather than trait samples.
- Clinical decision phenotypes are fractionating into data-driven subtypes: gambling disorder splits into impulsive vs emotionally vulnerable with blunted loss ERPs only in the latter (42779459); suicide attempt history separates loss-triggered disengagement from overpersistence in depression (42704954). Both need independent validation.
- Normative models of group decision-making under challenge: people prefer contractualist over utilitarian aggregation, and LLMs match neither (42759227).
- Human-AI teams entering classic group-psychology paradigms; hidden-profile information sharing improves with prompted inquiry in both mixed and human-only teams (42708610).
- Personal-consequence framings matching or beating collective-threat framings in health persuasion, with no trust penalty (42702911, antibiotics/UTI).
- Theoretical push to treat susceptibility to environmental influence on values as resource-rational rather than defective (42773691).
- Neglected-question reviews: joint/collaborative navigation almost unstudied despite being the default real-world case (42767709).
- Decision-quality differences by seizure focus laterality (42710371) and caregiver evaluation of irreversible surgical trade-offs (42730552) — small-N, flagged as suggestive.
