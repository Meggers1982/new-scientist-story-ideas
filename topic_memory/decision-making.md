# Topic Memory: decision-making
_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

## Established findings
- Computational decomposition (signal detection, drift-diffusion) repeatedly relocates apparent "deficits" or developmental change from perception/valuation to the decision rule — autism traits (42721846, 42721950, 42722131, Sep 2026) and now adolescent working memory, where drift rate and non-decision time, not puberty, carry the effect (42826555, Oct 2026).
- Steep delay discounting is a reliable correlate of harm but rarely the proximal cause: effects run through cognitive distortions in gamblers (42821202) and appear dose-dependently with brain structure only in daily cannabis users (42855476); earlier runs showed it in autistic traits (42722131).
- People do not aggregate conflicting judgments by averaging — they prefer contractualist solutions to utilitarian sums (42759227) and dyads pick a winner rather than splitting the difference (42822293).
- Confidence and accuracy dissociate systematically: feedback recalibrates certainty more than correctness (42734967); confidence tracks calories while accuracy sits with nutrition, and liking inflates it (42863397).

## Emerging threads
- Metacognitive "when to check" as the bottleneck in applied decisions: AI verification and failed generic warnings (42759086), self-directed sampling of negative drug anecdotes (42843138), labels reshaping recall (42832023).
- Motivation research fractionating: controllability separated from reward rate, with depressive traits sharpening sensitivity rather than blunting it (42675261); loss-triggered disengagement vs overpersistence in depression/suicide history (42704954).
- Incentive validity as an uncontrolled variable — points vs money changes effort, and household income moderates the value of a cash bonus (42475772). Watch whether this propagates into task-based clinical measurement.
- Symbolic vs costly signaling in politics: land acknowledgements shift awareness but not attitudes, and reward the institution (42791336); discrete emotions steer leader-face preference (42858097).
- Decision-making in regulatory/legal vacuums: MAiD eligibility judgments in the scanner (42091837), industry-written ketamine clinic guidelines with heavy conflicts of interest (42144140), ROPA IVF role allocation (42875086).
- Clinical subtyping of decision phenotypes still awaiting independent validation (42779459 gambling ERP subtypes; 42834510 wanting/liking gap in anorexia with suicidality).
- Judgment under genuine novelty and pressure: pilot knowledge elicitation on unrehearsable events (42095443); pressure as rule-complexity shift rather than resource drain (42875363) — both theory/qualitative, need data.
