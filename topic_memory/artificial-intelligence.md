# Topic Memory: artificial intelligence
_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

## Established findings
- Chatbots function as a de facto first-line health information service ahead of regulation: 66.5% of Nigerian HIV clinic patients used one for treatment questions (42736503, Sep 2026); abortion answers 65% acceptable but 8% complete (40550012, Sep 2026); AI therapy acceptance concentrates in the least trusting, most symptomatic users (41549774, Sep 2026).
- Access to AI tools is not itself an intervention — the routine or calibration around the tool carries the effect: metacognitive scaffolding beat autonomous AI use (42600450, Sep 2026); trust, not self-efficacy, predicts student reliance (42804850, Sep 2026).
- Clinical/behavioral ML performance is inflated without external validation: nonimprovement AUC 0.64–0.65 vs residual-state 0.73–0.77 (42117565); ergonomics models degraded unevenly on an independent sample (42669165, Aug 2026).
- Machines can expose the weakness of human reference standards: clinician weight bias in eating disorders (42068161); poor-to-fair inter-rater agreement on sleep endoscopy, with an untrained model at junior-clinician level (42766212, Sep 2026); sub-perceptual cortical disorganization in "normal" epilepsy tissue (42767799).

## Emerging threads
- AI as research apparatus, not object: synthetic faces indistinguishable from photographs used as stimuli (42773386), ChatGPT-assisted item selection for a new scale (42473812), spatial-statistical pathology (42767799) — validation standards lag badly.
- Synthetic social partners shift explicit attitudes but not implicit ones: AI playing a Chinese interlocutor moved stated attitudes and empathy, left IAT unchanged (42763964, Sep 2026); compare the no-judging-mind mechanism behind AI comfort (42691276).
- Disclosure asymmetry: people tell chatbots what they tell no one, and no system is designed to route them to a human (42298343, Sep 2026); developmental version in adolescents (42664282).
- Passive sensing as psychiatric measurement, still theoretical: Digital Leakage Effect maps DSM-5 criteria onto nocturnal phone signals and warns of Western-trained model bias (42753424, Sep 2026); free-text mood descriptions already match rating scales on real outcomes (40974258).
- Delegation/offloading as a loss mechanism — food-decision skill (42705481), ethical fading in negotiation (42697057), creative homogenization (42705114) — mostly conceptual, awaiting empirical tests.
- Public attitudes to AI are framing-sensitive: collective pronouns ("we") raise perceived AI threat (40745650).
- Machine consciousness moving to substrate arguments with practical stakes: Block's brain-waves-necessary claim vs computational counterarguments in the same BBS issue (42750504, Sep 2026).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-09: "AI as subtraction" — its measurable effects come from what it removes (clinician bias, fear of judgment; also contextual detail, idea diversity, decision skill, moral awareness, developmental experience). PMIDs 42691276, 42068161, 42671866, 42705114, 42705481, 42697057, 42664282, 42600450.
- 2026-09-29: "AI as the instrument" — generative models now supply the stimuli, items, ground truth and reference standards of behavioral and clinical research, with no validation or reporting standard. PMIDs 42773386, 42473812, 42767799, 42766212, 42669165, 42753424.
