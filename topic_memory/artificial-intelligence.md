## Emerging threads
- AI can look less biased than clinicians on structured tasks: ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude flagged eating disorders regardless of patient weight where clinicians did not (42068161, Sep 2026).
- AI support may work through absence of a judging mind, not presence of empathy — effect could self-cancel as expectations of AI empathy rise (42691276, Sep 2026).
- Preference for AI therapy concentrates in the least trusting, most symptomatic, insecurely attached users; therapy-experienced people prefer humans (41549774, Sep 2026).
- LLM summarization strips low-salience contextual detail that carries predictive signal; clinician-guided summaries did not help, bigger models did worse (42671866, Aug 2026).
- Delegation/offloading as a loss mechanism, so far mostly conceptual: food-decision skill erosion (42705481), ethical fading in negotiation (42697057), displaced adolescent developmental tasks (42664282) — all Aug–Sep 2026, no empirical tests yet.
- Individual creative gains vs collective homogenization of ideas; timing of AI entry and user expertise are the moderators (42705114, Sep 2026).
- AI tools alone are not an intervention in education; the scaffolding/metacognitive routine around them carries the effect (42600450, Sep 2026).
- Language as measurement: free-text descriptions of mood matched or beat rating scales on sick leave and healthcare use (40974258, Sep 2026) — watch for privacy/commercialization follow-ups (founder conflicts).
- Clinical prediction models predict states better than change: residual depression AUC 0.73–0.77 vs nonimprovement 0.64–0.65 (42117565, Sep 2026).
- Chatbot health information fails on completeness rather than accuracy — 65% acceptable, 8% complete on abortion questions, older model (40550012, Sep 2026).
- NLP/social-listening on patient forums as evidence infrastructure, with vendor-employed authors and proprietary tools (42673770, Aug 2026).
- Dark Tetrad: psychopathy and male sex prospectively predict generative-AI cyberbullying; sadism drops out once prior behavior is controlled (42374878, Sep 2026).

## Feature ideas already pitched
