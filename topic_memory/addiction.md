# Topic Memory: addiction
_Last updated: 2026-08-24_

## Established findings
- Behavioural/digital "addictions" (gaming, short-video, internet, exercise, love, AI use) are being measured almost entirely with cross-sectional self-report; conclusions are correlational and construct validity is contested (PMIDs 42374874, 42533520, 42364395, 41742036 — Aug 2026).
- Interpersonal factors — mimicry, peer norms, conflict, social withdrawal — recur as strong correlates of consumption and compulsive use across substances and behaviours (42427246, 42612478, 41742036, 42581660 — Aug 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Social neuroscience enters addiction: first hyperscanning study of abstinent heroin users shows disrupted brain-to-brain synchrony and reduced perspective-taking (41941931, Aug 2026). Watch for replication and longitudinal follow-up.
- Gambling risk migrating into gaming/esports culture even as overall teen gambling falls, including a new female risk profile (42599567); physical blind-box/trading-card products escape the loot-box regulatory debate entirely (42611551).
- "Backfire" findings on self-regulation: screen-time strategies associated with *worse* short-video addiction in low-inhibitory-control users (42374874); dual-pathway results where the same behaviour is protective and harmful (42374855).
- Food/sugar addiction: first real-time ecological momentary assessment of DSM-5 symptoms during sugar quitting; withdrawal-like profile in ~half of quitters, unresolved diagnostically (41905576).
- Dimensional deconstruction of core addiction constructs — impulsivity in cocaine use disorder splits into trait vs cognitive disinhibition with near-non-overlapping connectomes (42562118).
- Coercive/involuntary addiction treatment under scrutiny as jurisdictions debate expansion; 13-year Victorian audit shows 15% mortality at six months, minority benefit (42017394).
- Counter-narrative data on adolescent mental health: high natural remission of self-harm over two years, with gender-differentiated pathways (42633531).
