# Topic Memory: addiction
_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

## Established findings
- Behavioral/digital "addiction" constructs (gaming, short-video, internet, porn, AI, "digital addiction") rest overwhelmingly on cross-sectional self-report with assumed mediation direction; validity contested (42374874, 42533520, 42364395, 41742036 — Aug 2026; 42801890, 42785564, 42727287 — Sep 2026).
- Self-control/self-regulation is not uniformly protective: backfire and reversal effects now found in two independent samples (42374874 — Aug 2026; 42727287 — Sep 2026).
- Addiction neuroscience is moving beyond reward circuitry — brain-to-brain synchrony deficits, dimensional impulsivity connectomes, cue-driven collapse of somatosensory gating (41941931, 42562118 — Aug 2026; 42740334 — Sep 2026).
- Gambling evidence base broadening geographically beyond Western legal markets (42611551, 42599567 — Aug 2026; 41887939, 42704516 — Sep/Oct 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Repurposed pharmacology entering addiction: semaglutide blocking all three fentanyl reinstatement routes in rats (42228850), neuroimmune/inflammasome framework for relapse (42425280), psychedelics loosening perineuronal nets (41765343). All preclinical or review-level; watch for first human trials.
- Peripheral biomarkers in addiction psychiatry: plasma nectin-4 predicting one-year executive function in methamphetamine use disorder (42379379), unreplicated.
- Methods reckoning: RI-CLPM and model choice deflating the phone-causes-depression literature (42748880); network centrality routinely over-read as an intervention target (42612478, 42727287).
- Metacognitive beliefs outperforming emotion regulation as prospective predictors in gambling (42143790) — tension with Aug 2026 emotion-regulation-centered findings (42504448, 42533520).
- Perinatal thread: smoking–postpartum depression meta-analysis (42604887) and zuranolone post-marketing signals including breast milk exposure (42711976).
- Gambling risk migrating into gaming/esports and physical blind-box products outside loot-box regulation (42599567, 42611551).
- Coercive addiction treatment outcomes under scrutiny as jurisdictions debate expansion (42017394).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- Aug 2026: "The social brain of addiction" — mimicry, hyperscanning, conflict-centered symptom networks, hikikomori mediation, peer norms; argued the field measures the wrong unit of analysis. (NS Features, BBC Future, Knowable, Undark.)
- Oct 2026: Addiction medicine's import trade — GLP-1s, nectin-4, immunomodulators and psychedelics as borrowed treatments, with zuranolone's post-marketing record as the cautionary case. (NS Features, STAT, Nature, Undark.)
