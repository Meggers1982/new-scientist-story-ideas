# Topic Memory: autism
_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

## Established findings
- Deficit accounts in autism repeatedly weaken under tighter measurement: age-controlled imitation/face-looking (42462495), counterfactual reasoning intact but physically biased (41578156), matched perspective-taking with higher pupillary effort (41992731) — 2026-09.
- Autism is heavily entangled with intellectual disability services and psychotropic over-prescribing: >25% of ID polypharmacy patients above BNF chlorpromazine-equivalent ceiling, 42-58% with recorded autism (42670819) — 2026-09.
- Autism research remains dominated by Western, verbally fluent, self-selected online samples; non-Western data (Hong Kong masking 42522543; Brazil/Spain sexuality 42528308; UAE stigma 42556204) complicates existing consensus.

## Emerging threads
- Age at diagnosis may be a stronger genetic dividing line than sex; male-female genetic correlation high, questioning biological basis of the 4:1 ratio (42658390).
- Effort/cost as a hidden variable behind matched performance — pupillometry, masking, burnout (41992731, 42522543).
- Emotion regulation in young autistic children as a dyadic rather than individual property; treating parental stress moves dyadic but not solo measures (42665438).
- Empathy as risk amplifier rather than protective factor in socially withdrawn autistic youth (42670580).
- Under-recognized comorbidity: catatonia in 21.5% of autistic adolescents at a tertiary clinic, female-skewed (42610640).
- Passive vs active online use, not total screen time, tracks hikikomori-style withdrawal in autistic/ADHD young adults (42430259).
- Environmental/organizational determinants of "challenging behavior" — homelike design, care pathways — with a very thin evidence base (42605201).
- Body appreciation prospectively predicts self-esteem and flourishing in autistic adults; interoception as possible link to eating disorders (42566953).
- Group-by-brain interactions warn against pooled-sample biomarkers (42619183, very small N).
