# Topic Memory: autism
_Last updated: 2026-10-12_

## Established findings
- Deficit accounts weaken under tighter measurement or richer physiology: age-controlled imitation/face-looking (42462495), intact but physically biased counterfactuals (41578156), matched perspective-taking at higher pupillary cost (41992731), and now heightened affective/electrodermal response to others' distress with worse emotion labeling (42856069, 2026-10).
- The "autistic people lack empathy" trope is being replaced by an overwhelm/mismatch account across independent methods (42670580, 42856069).
- Autism research remains dominated by Western, verbally fluent, self-selected samples; non-Western and Gulf studies (42522543, 42528308, 42556204, 42790248) are the most novel and the least methodologically robust.
- Autism is deeply entangled with intellectual disability services and psychotropic over-prescribing (42670819).

## Emerging threads
- Autism neuroimaging heterogeneity: pooled-sample biomarkers are unsafe (42619183) and subtyping algorithms borrowed from neurodegeneration are being tried as a fix (42853485) — watch for independent replication outside ABIDE.
- Sex differences unresolved and contradictory: high male–female genetic correlation questioning the 4:1 ratio (42658390) versus boy-only prenatal biomarker associations (42843280).
- Prenatal/perinatal biology entering the digest: cord blood sEH running opposite to the neuroinflammation hypothesis (42843280); prenatal supplement evidence for preventing autism/ADHD judged inadequate (42849536).
- Interoception as the link between autism, body image and eating disorders (42566953), now extended beyond anorexia to binge-type eating disorders (42850402).
- Parent-as-delivery-mechanism interventions spreading from preschool emotion regulation (42665438) to transition-age vocational skills (42790248); outcomes still largely parent-reported.
- Under-recognized comorbidity and missed presentations: catatonia in autistic adolescents (42610640), binge eating (42850402).
- Caregiver burden may be front-loaded around diagnosis rather than cumulative (42853118, cross-sectional only).
