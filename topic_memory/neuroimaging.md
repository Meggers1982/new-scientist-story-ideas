# Topic Memory: neuroimaging
_Last updated: 2026-09-12_

## Established findings
- Structural MRI underperforms as a psychiatric mechanism-detector at scale: <1% mediation of trauma→bipolar (PMID 42234441), no reproducible structural ADHD subtypes (PMID 41989408), failed grey-matter neuroprotection replication (PMID 42623965). Now joined by weak effects in cortical covariance work (PMID 42176687, β=0.085).
- fMRI identifies individuals far better than it identifies disorders: fingerprinting 93–99% (PMID 42527301) versus gaming-disorder classification at 62.6% (PMID 42727812).
- Deliberate replication attempts are becoming a standard design in psychiatric imaging, with mixed survival rates (PMID 42044556, Sep 2026; PMID 42623965, Aug 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Transdiagnostic/normative modeling displacing patients-versus-controls: loneliness marker in dmPFC across schizophrenia, depression and Alzheimer's (PMID 42044556); ENIGMA Lifespan normative deviation applied to negative-symptom clusters (PMID 42176687); psychosis-risk reclassification (PMID 41823413).
- Behavioral-addiction neuroscience moving off the reward/dopamine script — serotonergic and GABAergic overlap in digital addiction (PMID 42190596), cerebellum as top discriminative region in gaming disorder (PMID 42727812). Watch whether this survives better-defined constructs.
- Machine learning reality checks: logistic regression matching gradient boosting and random forests on tabular clinical data (PMID 42634999); saliency-map attribution used as if it were neuroscientific evidence (PMID 42727812).
- Clinical tests misattributing the cause of failure — bvFTD memory scores with intact medial temporal lobe (PMID 40995692); functional seizures misread in genetic epilepsy (PMID 42704296); heritage-language children judged deficient while tracking word surprisal normally (PMID 42667920).
- Imaging access as a global-health story: cardiovascular and brain-malignancy excess deaths in rural Chinese epilepsy cohort implying undiagnosed structural lesions (PMID 42704311, Sep 2026).
- Metabolism–brain link maturing from spatial overlay to measured bioenergetics (PMID 42624217 → PMID 42669931), though evidence remains exploratory.
- LLM surprisal estimates becoming the default measuring instrument in neurolinguistics (PMID 42667920), alongside formal process models mapped to lesions (PMID 42527808).
- Imaging leaving the undergraduate pool: coal miners (PMID 42518290), anonymous organ donors (PMID 42667790), bilingual children (PMID 42667920).
- Small-N single-center positives still dominate the softer literature (trust game N=31, PMID 42341477; NODDI sleep apnea n≈24/group, PMID 42687385) — useful contrast cases for methods stories.

## Feature ideas already pitched
- "Brain imaging grows up": mega-consortium audits (ENIGMA/ABCD/GENFI) deflating structural-MRI psychiatry claims and the pivot to function, computation and network resilience — NS Features, Undark, The Atlantic, STAT, Nature (run 2026-08-23).
- "The test failed for the wrong reason": imaging as arbiter of what memory, seizure and language assessments actually measure, and the misdiagnosis and overtreatment that follow — NS Features, STAT, Guardian, Knowable (run 2026-09-12).
