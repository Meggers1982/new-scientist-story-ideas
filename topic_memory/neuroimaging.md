## Emerging threads
- Consortium-scale studies are repeatedly deflating structural-MRI claims in psychiatry: <1% mediation of trauma→bipolar (ENIGMA, PMID 42234441, Aug 2026); no reproducible structural ADHD subtypes in ABCD (PMID 41989408, Aug 2026); failed replication of grey-matter neuroprotection by cognitive enhancement therapy (PMID 42623965, Aug 2026). Watch whether functional/connectivity measures fare better.
- Reclassification pressure on psychosis risk: only COGDIS basic symptoms, not UHR criteria, produce a schizophrenia-like structural signature (PMID 41823413, Aug 2026). Watch for follow-up longitudinal or clinic-practice responses.
- Resilience as an active network property rather than "reserve": preserved functional integration in presymptomatic FTD mutation carriers despite atrophy (GENFI, PMID 41277710, Aug 2026).
- Computational/model-based imaging supplying mechanism where anatomy failed: valuation-circuit renormalisation predicts response to ablative neurosurgery in TRD (PMID 42533701); two-stage sentence-production model mapped to distinct lesion territory (PMID 42527808).
- Neuroimaging privacy/AI: transfer-learning connectome fingerprinting IDs individuals at up to 99% from <2 min of fMRI across unseen scanners (PMID 42527301, Jul 2026). Watch for governance responses on open repositories.
- Sample-representativeness problem surfacing empirically — coal miners vs students, prefrontal network efficiency d = −1.34 (PMID 42518290, Jul 2026); portable fNIRS pushing imaging out of the undergraduate pool.
- Under-covered clinical niches with imaging endpoints: intermittent luteal-phase escitalopram for PMDD irritability with insula reactivity (PMID 42515970); night-shift nurses' amygdala–metabolome–mood chain (PMID 42624217).
- Small-N single-centre positives still dominating the softer end of the literature (lucid dreaming structural traits PMID 41677447; footballer expertise PMID 42518012; sad-music ALE with only 9 studies PMID 42617440) — useful as contrast cases for methods stories.
