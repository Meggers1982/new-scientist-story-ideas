# Topic Memory: neuroimaging
_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

## Established findings
- Structural MRI underperforms as a psychiatric mechanism-detector at scale: <1% mediation of trauma→bipolar (PMID 42234441), no reproducible ADHD subtypes (PMID 41989408), failed grey-matter neuroprotection replication (PMID 42623965), tiny effects in cortical covariance work (PMID 42176687). Now reinforced by ABCD loneliness screen where 558 MRI measures lost to family income (PMID 42186177, Oct 2026).
- fMRI identifies individuals far better than it identifies disorders: fingerprinting 93–99% (PMID 42527301) versus gaming-disorder classification at 62.6% (PMID 42727812).
- Deliberate replication is now a standard design in psychiatric imaging, with mixed survival (PMID 42044556; PMID 42623965).
- Small-N single-center positives still dominate the soft literature (PMID 42341477, 42687385, 42458639, 42335494, 42746369) — reliable contrast cases for methods stories.

## Emerging threads
- Phenotype splitting as the precondition for any neural signal: hot vs cold suicidal ideation (PMID 42710718), physical vs social anhedonia (PMID 42746369), intrinsic vs extrinsic reward in gaming disorder (PMID 42335494), motivation vs expressivity in schizophrenia (PMID 42176687).
- Behavioral-addiction imaging off the dopamine script: serotonin/GABA overlap (PMID 42190596), cerebellum as top discriminator (PMID 42727812), intrinsic-reward distortion (PMID 42335494). Same senior author recurring (Guang-Heng Dong) — note dependence.
- Loneliness as a durable, transdiagnostic but weakly neural construct: dmPFC marker across three diagnoses (PMID 42044556), 71.6% persistence from age 10 to 14 (PMID 42186177), age inversion with young adults loneliest (PMID 42091836).
- Naturalistic neuroimaging revising lab-derived findings: movie free-viewing reveals orbitofrontal–anterior temporal gaze network (PMID 42772940); story-listening fNIRS in bilingual children (PMID 42667920).
- Night-time and state-dependent imaging: arterial spin labelling during actual sleep attempts in insomnia (PMID 42458639) versus daytime structural work (PMID 42687385).
- Cultural and ethical representativeness of imaging itself, not just recruitment breadth: Māori co-designed neuroimaging guidelines for Aotearoa New Zealand (PMID 42503436); links to Indigenous data sovereignty.
- Mechanism-first evolutionary/embodied models: freeze-arrest account of catatonia (PMID 42815707), number semantics in sensorimotor cortex (PMID 42764054), punishment sensitivity predicting patience (PMID 42800541).
- Machine learning and open-data reality checks: logistic regression matching ensembles (PMID 42634999), saliency maps treated as evidence (PMID 42727812), exploratory secondary analyses of shared OSF/OpenNeuro datasets (PMID 42091836).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- "Brain imaging grows up": consortium audits (ENIGMA/ABCD/GENFI) deflating structural-MRI psychiatry and the pivot to function and networks — NS Features, Undark, The Atlantic, STAT, Nature (run 2026-08-23).
- "The test failed for the wrong reason": imaging as arbiter of what memory, seizure and language assessments really measure, and the resulting misdiagnosis — NS Features, STAT, Guardian, Knowable (run 2026-09-12).
- "The questionnaire, not the scanner": symptom-scale coarseness as the hidden cause of psychiatric biomarker nulls; signal appears only after splitting the phenotype — NS Features, The Transmitter, Nature, Undark (run 2026-10-02).
