## Emerging threads
- Physiological "objective" measures underperform self-report for anxiety: electrodermal vs unpleasantness ratings in extinction (42546511, Sep 2026); HRV moving opposite to reported anxiety in autistic children (42632169); EEG alpha/theta losing to microstate transitions after adjusting for depression (42379500); resting HRV uninformative without a challenge condition (42302916).
- Corrective/null results against dominant narratives: flat THC dose-response for anxiety in infrequent users (42242480); pandemic did not uniformly worsen pre-existing illness, bipolar improved (42474420); high-anxious adults over-generalize fear but extinguish normally (42546510).
- Active controls matching bespoke interventions: expressive writing for trans adults, all three arms improved equally (42658633); waitlist-only comparator weakening a mechanism-tested app trial (42660574).
- Compressed/reframed treatment formats: Bergen 4-Day Treatment beating three-week inpatient OCD care (42500867); imagery rescripting for CBT-refractory hoarding (42462484).
- Anxiety as comorbidity marker in physical/endocrine disease: credibility-graded 48% anxiety in PCOS (42217371); attachment anxiety at 56 predicting frailty and MCI at 68 via stress and smoking (41885885).
- Dyadic and family framing of anxiety: coparent ADHD symptoms tracking postpartum mood (41982066); haptonomy beating VR for prenatal attachment (42610239).
- Candidate transdiagnostic constructs gaining traction: self-disgust (42497472), decentering (42660582), metacognitive beliefs (42656180), boredom (42675870).
