# Topic Memory: anxiety
_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

## Established findings
- Crisis does not uniformly worsen mental health: studies with genuine pre-crisis or prospective baselines repeatedly show stable or improving symptoms where cross-sectional surveys showed deterioration — pandemic review with bipolar improving (42474420, Sep 2026); Ukrainian civilians improving as objective threat rose (42831241, Oct 2026).
- Physiological "objective" markers underperform cheap self-report for anxiety: electrodermal vs unpleasantness ratings in extinction (42546511); HRV moving opposite to reported anxiety in autistic children (42632169); EEG alpha/theta losing to microstate transitions after adjusting for depression (42379500); resting HRV uninformative without a challenge (42302916) — all Sep 2026.

## Emerging threads
- Self-report's own validity problem: falling symptom scores under sustained threat may reflect recalibration, response shift or differential attrition rather than recovery (42831241) — a caution on the thread above.
- Sleep disturbance as the hinge that blocks adaptation/recovery (42831241; 42302916).
- Macro-social forces registering as individual anxiety: war (42831241), post-consent-reform sexual norms (42844656), pronatalism and mortality salience (42833108). New register for this topic as of Oct 2026 — watch whether it recurs.
- Replication-fragile paradigms still generating headline claims: terror management / mortality salience (42833108), against a batch of corrective nulls (42546511, 42546510, 42658633, 42379500).
- Active controls matching bespoke interventions (42658633; waitlist-only comparator in 42660574).
- Compressed or reframed treatment formats: Bergen 4-Day Treatment vs three-week inpatient OCD care (42500867); imagery rescripting for CBT-refractory hoarding (42462484).
- Anxiety as comorbidity marker in physical/endocrine disease: 48% anxiety in PCOS (42217371); attachment anxiety at 56 predicting frailty and MCI at 68 (41885885).
- Candidate transdiagnostic constructs: self-disgust (42497472), decentering (42660582), metacognitive beliefs (42656180), boredom (42675870).
- Qualitative work entering the anxiety stream (42844656) — moral rather than physical performance anxiety in young men; worth watching for quantitative follow-up.

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-01: "The objective-measure problem in anxiety science" — EDA, HRV and EEG band power failing against self-report, tied to wearable stress scores and neurofeedback headsets (42546511, 42632169, 42379500, 42302916).
- 2026-10-11: "Recalibrating the fear baseline" — anxiety moving opposite to objective conditions across war, consent culture and death-anxiety/fertility work; adaptation vs wellbeing, sleep as the blocker (42831241, 42844656, 42833108).
