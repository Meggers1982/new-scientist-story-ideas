# Topic Memory: emotion regulation
_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

## Established findings
- Cognitive reappraisal underperforms outside the lab: rarely used and unlinked to mood in depression (42660575), null for activity levels (42413746) and parent/toddler screen use (42447742); now complicated further by punishment-sensitive people having MORE right dlPFC gray matter yet reappraising worse (42722323, Sep 2026).
- Habitual expressive suppression is the recurring cost-bearing strategy across unrelated domains: toddler screen time (42447742), problematic porn use (42766058), parental burnout in combat-deployed fathers (42671804), adolescent family conflict (42753047).
- Interpersonal regulation is potent and measurable: another person's reframing lowers startle more than self-reframing (42704885), active listening amplifies reappraisal with brain-to-brain synchrony (42288208), mid-conflict validation shifts a partner within seconds (42441643).
- Within-person state beats stable trait as a predictor: impulsivity deviation from personal baseline predicts suicidal ideation where trait impulsivity does not (42778027); same pattern in depression EMA (42660575, 41885910).
- Self-reported regulation deficits diverge from performance measures (42704885) — a standing caution, since most new work remains entirely self-report.

## Emerging threads
- Buffers saturate: resilience prevents but does not reverse adolescent distress (42749162); unit support fails at high combat exposure (42671804); meta-humanization works best at moderate threat (42723350).
- Emotion regulation as scalable intervention in low-resource settings: lay-delivered caregiver program halving violence against children in a displacement setting (42754240); Urdu Unified Protocol feasibility RCT (42768329).
- Bodily/contemplative routes: HRV biofeedback alters amygdala–mPFC structural covariance (42669395); six-month randomized TM workplace trial (42778032); first cross-tradition review of prayer, chanting and meditation neuroimaging (42670176).
- LLMs entering the field as both tool and manipulation — narrative coherence plus personalization is the active ingredient; fragmented personalization backfires (42714089).
- Deliberate trait change: neuroticism shifting in 10 weeks of internet affect phobia therapy via defense maturity (42735080, uncontrolled pilot).
- Method turn toward state-dependent and dyadic measurement: mood-induction fMRI (42773324); response-surface analysis of parent–adolescent perception mismatch (42753047); home audio and second-by-second coding (41642821, 42269373).
- Intergroup emotion regulation, with disgust rather than empathy as the lever on polarization (42723350).

## Feature ideas already pitched
