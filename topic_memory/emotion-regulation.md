# Topic Memory: emotion regulation
_Last updated: 2026-09-08_

## Established findings
- Cognitive reappraisal underperforms outside the lab: rarely deployed and unlinked to mood in depression (42660575, Sep 2026), null across activity levels (42413746) and parent/toddler screen use (42447742); only situation selection and reinterpretation predicted lower daily negative affect (41806361).
- Interpersonal regulation is potent and measurable: another person's reframing lowers startle more than self-reframing (42704885), active listening amplifies subsequent reappraisal with brain-to-brain synchrony (42288208), mid-conflict validation shifts a partner out of negative affect within seconds (42441643).
- Self-reported regulation deficits diverge from performance: FND patients report severe difficulty but show no lab differences (42704885).
- Attentional distraction/disengagement has a cost — blocks habituation and impairs memory for the stimulus (42693580).

## Emerging threads
- Bodily and autonomic routes into regulation: HRV biofeedback alters amygdala–mPFC structural covariance (42669395); interoceptive sensibility, not cognitive strategy, tracks physical activity (42413746); VR mindfulness shifts children's HRV, uncontrolled (41484577).
- "Polyregulation" — strategy combinations, not single techniques, as the unit of analysis (41806361).
- Second-by-second co-regulation methods spreading into families: infant heart rate with mothers, fathers and triads (42269373), 16 hours of home audio to catch mothers snapping (41642821).
- Positive-emotion regulation as its own literature with different rules: dampening as contrast avoidance with a real short-term payoff (42468402); savoring works equally on high-arousal highs (42398883).
- Social context over trauma history: post-migration social stress, not trauma exposure, severs refugees from interpersonal regulation (42619365).
- Dimensional/neural splitting of childhood dysregulation: tonic vs phasic irritability (42702353); sensory features predicting change in autistic self-injury (42657937).
