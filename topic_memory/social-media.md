# Topic Memory: social media
_Last updated: 2026-08-25_

## Established findings
- Feed content exposure predicts worse body image (thinspiration → dissatisfaction; fitspiration → lower appreciation), with little support for the reverse "vulnerable people seek it out" path and no gender moderation (PMID 42537570, Jul 2026).
- Social media exposure to appearance ideals affects adults beyond students/teens, e.g. prospective disordered eating in working nurses via body dissatisfaction (PMID 41968956, Aug 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Objective measurement (wearable cameras, heart rate, experience sampling, peer nomination) is contradicting self-report-era conclusions — e.g. lower heart rate during pre-bed screen use, displacement rather than arousal (PMID 41556268); peer-nominated "school influencers" high in both prosocial behavior and aggression (PMID 42552601).
- Reversed causal arrows: within-person models leave distress/sleepiness → problematic phone use, not the reverse (PMID 42636649, Aug 2026).
- Mechanism shifting from restraint failure to metacognition and initiation: overestimated self-control drives compulsive short-video use (PMID 42608804); initiatory self-control is the transdiagnostic common factor (PMID 42285011).
- "Entrapment" framings of technology distress rather than addiction — Digital Coercion Scale in psychiatric inpatients (PMID 42585824).
- Online communities as clinical evidence sources and risk vectors: Reddit surfacing undocumented "extreme hunger" in anorexia recovery (PMID 41983301); GLP-1/Ozempic normalized in edtwt with only 3.5% risk content (PMID 42604953).
- Platform features under-tested: adding peer networking to an eating-disorder app gave no benefit (PMID 41992076); a WeChat mini-program writing intervention worked as well in its simple as its elaborate form (PMID 42635256).
- Cultural and contextual moderators: Wayuu focus groups suggest collective cultural identity buffers globalized appearance ideals arriving with connectivity (PMID 42520348).
- New corpora replacing stale datasets: real court-file grooming conversations vs. 20-year-old decoy transcripts (PMID 42427109).
