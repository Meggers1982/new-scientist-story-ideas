# Topic Memory: social media
_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- Feed content exposure worsens body image regardless of whether the body is real or whether the viewer habitually compares (42537570 Jul 2026; 42379075 Sep 2026; 41968956 Aug 2026; 41580900 Sep 2026); new producer-side evidence shows fitness creators caught in the same machinery (42785604 Sep 2026).
- Total/problematic use loses explanatory power once specific states or behaviors are modeled: cybervictimization and metacognition over problematic use (42714373); distress over attachment style (42388174); active engagement over passive exposure for vaping (42229276 Oct 2026).
- Composite constructs resolve into informative sub-parts: FOMO as brooding not reflection (41622812); main character syndrome as identity diffusion not narcissism (42696901); muscle dysmorphia harm in Appearance Intolerance not Drive for Size (42809933).
- Online forums function as an unlicensed clinical evidence base ahead of professional guidance (41983301, 42604953, 42704210).

## Emerging threads
- Internet-coined folk diagnoses being formalized into scales, clinical presentations and policy arguments (42696901, 42296638, 42817062, 42808340, 41770174) — check construct validity and who validates.
- Non-screen upstream stressors (school burnout, parental emotion socialization) as entry points into compulsive use models, both from Tianjin Normal University (41580900, 42817062).
- Synthetic personalities: harm transfers from human to virtual influencers, warmth does not — self-disclosure backfires unless the avatar is attractive (42379075, 42765465).
- Algorithms inflate the tails of social information while generative AI collapses it to the mode; the same loop now applied to psychiatric vocabulary (42715802, 41770174).
- Asymmetry in persuasion: misinformation exposure shifts attitudes via traceable heuristics (42641053) while source-authority badging on AI corrections moves trust but not belief (42765662).
- Spillover of digital-habit research into non-mental-health domains: cybersecurity risk (42808340), ad avoidance and transparency regulation (42790213).
- FOMO hub cluster of Sep 2026 (42722509, 41622812, 42594777) did not recur in Oct 2026 — may have been a one-month artefact.
