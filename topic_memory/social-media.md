# Topic Memory: social media
_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Established findings
- Feed content exposure worsens body image, with little support for self-selection and no gender or comparison-habit moderation; holds for synthetic bodies too (PMIDs 42537570 Jul 2026; 42379075 Sep 2026; 41968956 Aug 2026; 41580900 Sep 2026).
- Measures of total/problematic use lose explanatory power once specific states are modeled: cybervictimization and metacognitive beliefs over problematic use in depressed teens (42714373); distress over attachment style (42388174); overestimated self-control over content pull (42608804).
- Online forums function as an unlicensed clinical evidence base, documenting phenomena ahead of professionals (41983301, 42604953, 42704210).

## Emerging threads
- FOMO as a hub mediator across vicarious trauma, brooding and impulsive buying (42722509, 41622812, 42594777) — watch for longitudinal or behavioral confirmation.
- Problematic phone use may be two phenomena: "ritualized" contentless looping vs content-driven absorption, U-shaped with mind-wandering (42492199).
- Engagement signals misread by platforms and researchers alike: overmoralization penalty (42686956); likes used as weapons (42424914).
- Algorithms inflate the tails of social information while generative AI collapses it to the mode — one mechanism, two distortions (42715802); relates to "synthetic consensus" in radicalization (41869924).
- Objective/within-person methods reversing self-report-era conclusions (41556268, 42636649, 42552601).
- Entrapment rather than addiction as a framing of technology distress (42585824).
- Social commerce as mood repair — consumer-protection angle newly in play (42594777).
- Randomized tests of health misinformation mechanisms, e.g. IUD scare content via availability then affect heuristics (42641053).
