# Topic Memory: exercise
_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

## Established findings
- Volume of activity is a poor unit for mental health benefit: dose ceilings and non-monotonic curves recur (PMIDs 42412001, 42605073, Aug 2026), and guidance/instruction outpredicts minutes accumulated in older adults (PMID 42807680, Sep 2026).
- Clinical populations are markedly sedentary and the exercise evidence/treatment base for them is thin or absent: bipolar disorder (PMID 42545207), eating disorder services with no compulsive-exercise protocol (PMID 41982074), refractory epilepsy and dissociative seizures (PMID 42700754).
- Exercise-psychology trials in this literature are dominated by small, unblinded, self-report designs; nulls and failed mediation are now routinely published by the originating labs (PMIDs 42579016, 42615122, 42526739, 42714131, 42772728, 42801814).

## Emerging threads
- Minimum effective dose: 3-minute daily micro-goals outperform two 30-minute sessions via ease of compliance (PMID 42704303); ~10 min/day of "exercise snacks" lowers blood pressure and perceived stress with no BMI change (PMID 42810116).
- Motivation carries a body-image bill: social-comparison feedback raises effort and lowers body satisfaction in the same session (PMID 42805545); "realistic" postpartum imagery backfires (PMID 41724692).
- Moralization of health behavior is driven by perceived interpersonal harm, not disgust (PMID 41185114) — direct implications for campaign framing and stigma.
- Digital access as a gate on exercise benefit: internet use and digital self-efficacy condition who receives guidance (PMID 42807680); fitness Instagram correlates with activity only in non-identifying exercisers (PMID 42803216).
- Instruction method as an active ingredient: internal-sensation cueing beat demonstration on fitness and kinematics over 16 weeks (PMID 42804848).
- Social ingredients weaker than assumed: group identification did not precede self-efficacy in runners (PMID 42722364); no sport-type difference in post-exercise affect, though underpowered (PMID 42801814); contrast with 28-year loneliness cohort (PMID 42544718).
- Methods correction spreading: random-intercept cross-lagged models and explicit equivalence testing deflating prior directional and "no difference" claims.
