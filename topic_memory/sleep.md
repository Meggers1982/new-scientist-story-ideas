# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-09-20_

## Established findings
- The mortality/inflammation signal in sleep duration sits at the long end, not the short: ≥9 h linked to 17% higher all-cause mortality over 30.8 years (PMID 42744622, Sep 2026) and to elevated inflammatory indices (PMID 42546640, Jul 2026); <6 h nulls after adjustment in both.
- Sleep quality and multi-night accumulation outperform duration for mood (PMID 42617377, Aug 2026) and cardiometabolic risk burden (PMID 42648984, Sep 2026).
- Sleep is unevenly distributed by social position, not merely by choice: intersectional MAIHDA analysis of 235,326 US adults finds both short and long sleep concentrated in multiply marginalized groups (PMID 42660768, Sep 2026); qualitative "hustle culture" mechanism (PMID 42624738); shift work and toddler bedtime routines buffered by social capital (PMID 42545383).
- Sleep problems mark severe depressive phenotypes: ~50% of depressive episodes meet nightmare-disorder criteria with actigraphy-confirmed fragmentation (PMID 42617369, Aug 2026).

## Emerging threads
- Composite scores hide separable patterns: bedtime vs wake-time regularity diverge in autistic children (PMID 42593049); latent profile analysis of Life's Essential 8 isolates a distinct poor-sleep/low-activity cluster tracking bipolar risk (PMID 42723029).
- Proxy–outcome dissociation across sleep instrumentation: ECG-AI reads autonomic response not airway (PMID 42747635); PTT-derived overnight blood pressure (PMID 42754769); spike-wave index improves while function does not (PMID 42407303).
- Directionality re-tested with lagged/genetic designs and coming out one-way: sleep→depression (PMID 42541934); PTSD→OSA (PMID 42611531).
- Trait "bad sleepers" may not be: extreme evening chronotypes differ from larks only in phase, not sleep quality or rhythm strength (PMID 42751860).
- Consumer devices as research instruments — computer-vision crib monitors supplying nightly infant sleep data at scale, with affluence-skewed samples (PMID 42470789).
- Family/vulnerable-population sleep interventions gaining attention: sleep-training dropout experience (PMID 42725637), trauma-informed telehealth program for foster carers (PMID 42725804).
- Under-covered for general readers: chronotherapeutics and eNAMPT (PMID 41903946); lucidity vs dream control distinction with no standardized control measure (PMID 42728107); invertebrate sleep as a template for catastrophic forgetting in AI (PMID 42728400).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-08-22: "The sleep measurement crisis" — hours-slept failing as master metric vs multi-night quality, regularity asymmetry, U-shaped duration risk; unvalidated consumer sleep scores (PMIDs 42617377, 42546640, 42593049, 42541934, 42628578, 42495965). Outlets: NS Features, Wired, Undark, STAT.
- 2026-09-20: "What sleep instruments actually detect" — proxy chains and biomarker–outcome dissociation across ECG-AI apnea detection, pulse transit time, spike-wave index, actigraphy, crib computer vision (PMIDs 42747635, 42754769, 42407303, 42637627, 42470789, 42648984, 42751860). Outlets: NS Features, STAT, MIT Technology Review, Undark.
- Not yet pitched, still live: sleep as a structurally rationed resource (inequity/shift work/institutional waking); UK Biobank underpowered for rare psychiatric outcomes.
