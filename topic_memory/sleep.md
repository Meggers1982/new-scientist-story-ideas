# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-10-10_

## Established findings
- Sleep duration's mortality and inflammation signal sits at the long end, not the short: ≥9 h linked to 17% higher all-cause mortality over 30.8 years (PMID 42744622, Sep 2026) and elevated inflammatory indices (PMID 42546640, Jul 2026); <6 h nulls after adjustment.
- Quality, fragmentation and multi-night accumulation outperform duration for mood (PMID 42617377) and cardiometabolic burden (PMID 42648984) — and "quality" itself is not one construct: identical insomnia symptoms can accompany opposite subjective experiences (PMID 42838779, Oct 2026); extreme chronotypes differ in phase, not quality (PMID 42751860).
- Sleep is unevenly distributed by social position: intersectional MAIHDA analysis of 235,326 US adults (PMID 42660768), hustle-culture rationing (PMID 42624738), shift work and toddler bedtime routines (PMID 42545383).
- Sleep disturbance marks severe psychiatric phenotypes: ~50% of depressive episodes meet nightmare-disorder criteria (PMID 42617369); daily reciprocal loop with restriction, binge eating and suicidal ideation in anorexia spectrum disorders (PMID 42834479, Oct 2026).
- Person-centered clustering beats composite scoring: Life's Essential 8 latent profiles isolate a poor-sleep/low-activity cluster tracking bipolar risk (PMID 42723029); three-class sleep typology in older Vietnamese adults (PMID 42838779).

## Emerging threads
- Proxy–outcome dissociation across sleep instrumentation: ECG-AI reads autonomic response not airway (PMID 42747635); pulse-transit-time blood pressure (PMID 42754769); spike-wave index improves while function does not (PMID 42407303).
- Measurement base is swinging back toward self-report: the Oct 2026 batch is entirely self-reported, including intensive EMA (PMID 42834479), after an instrumentation-heavy September.
- Directionality unsettled: lagged/genetic designs had favored one-way sleep→symptom effects (PMID 42541934), but within-person daily data show reciprocal loops (PMID 42834479).
- Self-medication and the CBT-I access gap: 26.7% of Spanish drug-survey respondents using drugs for sleep (PMID 42802253); medication use distinguishing severe sleep class (PMID 42838779).
- AI moving from instrument/analogy (PMIDs 42747635, 42728400) to psychological exposure — AI competence anxiety reaching sleep via rumination (PMID 42830665).
- Consumer and smartphone platforms as research instruments: computer-vision crib monitors (PMID 42470789), ecological momentary assessment replacing clinic questionnaires in psychiatry (PMID 42834479).
- Sleep-dependent consolidation gaining specificity: binding of incidental multidimensional context (PMID 42844862), alongside invertebrate work framed around catastrophic forgetting (PMID 42728400).
- Under-covered: lucidity vs dream control, with no standardized control measure (PMID 42728107); family/vulnerable-population interventions (PMIDs 42725637, 42725804).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-08-22: "The sleep measurement crisis" — hours-slept failing as master metric; unvalidated consumer sleep scores (PMIDs 42617377, 42546640, 42593049, 42541934, 42628578, 42495965).
- 2026-09-20: "What sleep instruments actually detect" — proxy chains and biomarker–outcome dissociation (PMIDs 42747635, 42754769, 42407303, 42637627, 42470789, 42648984, 42751860).
- 2026-10-10: "The informal economy of sleep" — self-medication, routine and religion filling the CBT-I access gap, with rumination as the shared mechanism (PMIDs 42802253, 42838779, 42830665, 42834479). Outlets: NS Features, Guardian science, STAT, Undark.
- Not yet pitched, still live: sleep as a structurally rationed resource (inequity/shift work/institutional waking); UK Biobank underpowered for rare psychiatric outcomes; EMA's reshaping of psychiatric measurement.
