# Topic Memory: trauma
_Last updated: 2026-09-04_

## Established findings
- Childhood adversity/maltreatment is a robust predictor of symptom severity and persistence across very different outcomes: persistence of common mental disorders (42687372, 2026-09), depression severity in first-episode drug-naïve patients (42673683), baseline distress in chronic pain rehab (42166956).
- Trauma-related diagnosis retains independent predictive value even when categorical diagnoses generally fail to predict outcomes (42644771).

## Emerging threads
- Instability of the PTSD construct: cluster-model choice swings prevalence 18–38% (42635035); avoidance a weak indicator in refugees (42635367); non-Criterion-A minority stressors track PTSS at moderate magnitudes (42658623); screening gaps in the perinatal period (41936021).
- Trauma constructs leaking into legal decision-making: judges using demeanor, emotionality and "signs of trauma" in 518 sexual assault rulings (41308177); therapy prompts reshaping narrative coherence with asylum implications (42684797).
- Early adversity sets the starting point, not the slope of recovery — high-ACE pain patients improved as much as others (42166956); dropout from inpatient trauma treatment unpredicted by symptom severity, returnees did as well as stayers (42222912).
- Mechanism papers separating self-concept from behavior: "trauma identity" vs safety-seeking after the 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquakes (42036873); perpetration-based moral injury linked specifically to depersonalization/derealization in Israeli veterans (41347711).
- Directionality reversals: depression preceding and feeding complex PTSD/DSO in unattended children in China (42594693).
- Understudied populations entering the trauma literature: de facto unattended children in China (42594693), children of substance-using fathers in Lahore slums (41974448), refugee young adults in Sweden (42635367).
- Weakening biomarker story: hs-CRP showed no independent association with depression severity in a first-episode, never-medicated sample; BMI was the main CRP correlate (42673683).
- Intervention at the acute window: feasibility of a 17-minute video plus text program delivered during sexual assault forensic exams (42635087).
- Memory under real extreme threat: mass shooting survivors report time slowing and sequence gaps, with encoding organized around escape routes (42647049).
